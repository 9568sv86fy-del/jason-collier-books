    Old ManOn The Mountain

    Seven days. One bull. The mountain is not empty.

    You are Harlan Wade, sixty-eight, walking into the high country with your father’s rifle. Tag the old bull. Be off the mountain before the seventh day ends. Keep a fire when the dark comes.

      How to hunt

      Seven days on the ridge

      TrailFollow the orange blazes: Trailhead, Ridge, Meadow, Creek, Dark Timber, Camp. Side trails split off to the Seep and the High Basin. DayEach day runs dawn to night in a few minutes. The sky tells you. Sleep at the tent, or stay up and pay for it. FirePick up dead wood. Feed the ring at camp. Nights without a flame grow cold, and the timber comes in close. GlassIn open country, raise the binoculars. The old bull is heavy, pale in the rack, with a notched brow. A younger bull is lighter and even. CamerasYou carry two. Hang them, give them time, then check the card. Elk, mule deer, or a smudge that should not be standing there. SignStep off your hurry. Tracks, a broken limb, claw marks, a place where the noise stops. Look, or tap the ground beside them. The shotGet still. When he is close, aim, and fire as the sight crosses the notch. Then get down to the truck. The darkIf it finds you, run and jump the deadfall. You do not shoot this thing.
